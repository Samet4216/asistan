## 🎯 Problem Formulation
Convolutional networks possess strong inductive biases (translation equivariance and locality) which restrict scaling on massive datasets (JFT-300M).

## ⚙️ Core Architectural Contribution
Vision Transformer (ViT) splits images into 16x16 patches, applies linear projections, prepends a learnable class token, and processes the sequence with standard Transformer encoder blocks.
