# Paper Card: RepVGG: Making VGG-style ConvNets Great Again (2021)

**arXiv ID:** `2101.03697` | **Domain:** Structural Re-parameterization
**Keywords:** repvgg, re-parameterization, inference-acceleration, vgg, residual-fusion

## 🎯 Problem Statement & Motivation
Proposes structural re-parameterization: training a multi-branch network with residual branches and fusing them algebraically at inference time into a plain 3x3 feedforward ConvNet that maximizes memory locality and throughput on GPUs.
