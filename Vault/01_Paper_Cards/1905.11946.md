## 🎯 Problem Statement & Motivation
Demonstrates that balancing network depth, width, and resolution using a principled compound scaling coefficient alpha * beta^2 * gamma^2 ~= 2 achieves state-of-the-art accuracy with up to 8.4x smaller parameter counts.
