## 🎯 Problem Formulation
Standard SGD has uniform learning rates for all coordinates and struggles with sparse gradients, noisy objectives, and non-stationary loss surfaces.

## ⚙️ Core Architectural Contribution
Adaptive Moment Estimation (Adam) combines exponential moving averages of gradients (first moment) and squared gradients (second moment), with bias correction terms for early training steps.

## 📐 Mathematical Formulations
$$
\hat{m}_t = \frac{m_t}{1 - \beta_1^t}, \quad \hat{v}_t = \frac{v_t}{1 - \beta_2^t}
$$
$$
\theta_t = \theta_{t-1} - \frac{\alpha}{\sqrt{\hat{v}_t} + \epsilon} \hat{m}_t
$$
