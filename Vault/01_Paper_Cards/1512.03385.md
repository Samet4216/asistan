## 🎯 Problem Formulation
Deeper neural networks suffer from the degradation problem: as depth increases, accuracy saturates and degrades rapidly due to optimization difficulties rather than overfitting.

## ⚙️ Core Architectural Contribution
Residual neural networks (ResNet) featuring explicit identity shortcut connections that allow layers to learn residual mappings $\mathcal{F}(x) = \mathcal{H}(x) - x$ rather than unreferenced mappings.

## 📐 Mathematical Formulations
$$
y = \mathcal{F}(x, \{W_i\}) + x

$$
