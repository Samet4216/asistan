## 🎯 Problem Formulation
Indiscriminately retrieving documents regardless of whether external knowledge is needed harms generation fluency and introduces unhelpful or distracting context.

## ⚙️ Core Architectural Contribution
Self-RAG trains a single language model that generates special reflection tokens (`[Retrieve]`, `[IsRel]`, `[IsSup]`, `[IsUse]`) to dynamically decide when to retrieve, critique passages, and verify factual grounding.
