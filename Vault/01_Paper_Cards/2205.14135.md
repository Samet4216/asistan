## 🎯 Problem Formulation
Standard attention algorithm is memory-bound (IO-bound) rather than compute-bound, frequently reading and writing the $N \times N$ attention matrix to slow GPU High-Bandwidth Memory (HBM).

## ⚙️ Core Architectural Contribution
FlashAttention computes exact attention with IO-awareness by splitting queries, keys, and values into blocks that fit within fast on-chip SRAM, computing online softmax updates without materializing the $N \times N$ matrix in HBM.

## 📐 Mathematical Formulations
$$
m_{\text{new}} = \max(m_{\text{prev}}, m_{\text{block}})
$$
$$
\ell_{\text{new}} = e^{m_{\text{prev}} - m_{\text{new}}} \ell_{\text{prev}} + e^{m_{\text{block}} - m_{\text{new}}} \ell_{\text{block}}
$$
