# Paper Card: Direct Preference Optimization: Your Language Model is Secretly a Reward Model (2023)

## 🎯 Problem Formulation
RLHF with PPO requires training a separate reward model, sampling autoregressive generations during training, and complex multi-model coordination in VRAM.

## ⚙️ Core Architectural Contribution
Direct Preference Optimization (DPO) algebraically reparameterizes the reward in the RLHF objective directly as the log ratio of policy probabilities, solving alignment via simple binary cross-entropy.

## 📐 Mathematical Formulations
$$
r(x, y) = \beta \log \frac{\pi_\theta(y|x)}{\pi_{\text{ref}}(y|x)}
$$
$$
\mathcal{L}_{\text{DPO}} = -\mathbb{E} \left[ \log \sigma \left( \beta \log \frac{\pi_\theta(y_w|x)}{\pi_{\text{ref}}(y_w|x)} - \beta \log \frac{\pi_\theta(y_l|x)}{\pi_{\text{ref}}(y_l|x)} \right) \right]
$$
