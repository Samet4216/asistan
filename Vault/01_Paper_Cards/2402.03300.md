# Paper Card: DeepSeekMath & GRPO: Group Relative Policy Optimization (2024)

**arXiv ID:** `2402.03300` | **Domain:** Critic-Free RL & Policy Optimization
**Keywords:** grpo, group-relative-policy-optimization, reinforcement-learning, deepseekmath, critic-free, llm-reasoning

## 🎯 Problem Statement & Motivation
Introduces Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO), eliminating the separate memory-heavy Critic/Value network of PPO by generating a group of responses per prompt and computing relative advantage baselines across the group, dramatically reducing training VRAM while optimizing complex mathematical and reasoning policies.
