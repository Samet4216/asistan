# Paper Card: Mastering Diverse Domains through World Models (DreamerV3) (2023)

**arXiv ID:** `2301.04104` | **Domain:** Model-Based Reinforcement Learning & World Models
**Keywords:** world-models, dreamerv3, model-based-rl, recurrent-state-space-model, rssm, imagination-planning, sample-efficiency

## 🎯 Problem Statement & Motivation
DreamerV3 builds a generative recurrent world model (RSSM) of the environment and trains actor-critic policies purely within imagined latent rollouts, solving Atari, continuous control, and Minecraft from scratch without human data or domain-specific hyperparameter tuning.
