## 🎯 Problem Formulation
Recurrent neural networks and LSTMs suffer from sequential computational bottlenecks ($O(N)$ sequential operations) and difficulty learning long-range dependencies.

## ⚙️ Core Architectural Contribution
The Transformer architecture, relying solely on multi-head scaled dot-product attention without recurrence or convolutions, enabling full parallelization across sequence tokens.

## 📐 Mathematical Formulations
$$
\text{Attention}(Q, K, V) = \text{softmax}\left(\frac{QK^T}{\sqrt{d_k}}\right)V
$$
$$
\text{MultiHead}(Q, K, V) = \text{Concat}(\text{head}_1, \dots, \text{head}_h)W^O
$$
