# Paper Card: Playing Atari with Deep Reinforcement Learning (2013)

**arXiv ID:** `[[1312.5602]]` | **Year:** `2013`

### 🌐 Synaptic Knowledge Graph Links

## 🎯 Problem Formulation
Reinforcement learning with non-linear neural function approximators is prone to divergence due to correlated data samples and non-stationary target distributions.

## ⚙️ Core Architectural Contribution
Deep Q-Networks (DQN) combining deep convolutional networks with an Experience Replay memory buffer that breaks data correlation and stabilizes gradient updates.

## 📐 Mathematical Formulations
$$
Q^*(s, a) = \mathbb{E}_{s'} [r + \gamma \max_{a'} Q^*(s', a') \mid s, a]
$$
$$
L_i(\theta_i) = \mathbb{E}_{(s,a,r,s')} [(r + \gamma \max_{a'} Q(s', a'; \theta_i^-) - Q(s, a; \theta_i))^2]
$$
