## 🎯 Problem Formulation
Standard policy gradient methods are sensitive to step sizes; large updates ruin policy performance, while TRPO is computationally complex and requires second-order conjugate gradient inversions.

## ⚙️ Core Architectural Contribution
Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO) using a novel clipped surrogate objective function that penalizes policy changes moving the probability ratio away from 1.

## 📐 Mathematical Formulations
$$
r_t(\theta) = \frac{\pi_\theta(a_t|s_t)}{\pi_{\theta_{\text{old}}}(a_t|s_t)}
$$
$$
L^{\text{CLIP}}(\theta) = \hat{\mathbb{E}}_t \left[ \min(r_t(\theta)\hat{A}_t, \; \text{clip}(r_t(\theta), 1-\epsilon, 1+\epsilon)\hat{A}_t) \right]
$$
