# Paper Card: Addressing Function Approximation Error in Actor-Critic Methods (TD3) (2018)

## 🎯 Problem Statement & Motivation
Proposes Twin Delayed DDPG (TD3) to tackle function approximation error and overestimation bias in actor-critic methods by introducing clipped double Q-learning, delayed policy updates, and target policy smoothing regularization.
